● Pen Detector → ESP8266 LED

Watches your camera feed in the browser. When the target object is in view, it signals your ESP8266 to light its built-in LED — and turns it off again once the object is gone.

⚠️
Open this file directly on your phone or computer (double-tap it, or drag it into your browser) instead of a hosted preview. Camera access and requests to your ESP8266 over your home network both require that.
